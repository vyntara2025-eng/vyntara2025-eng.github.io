Privacy Policy

Vyntara Solutions (Pty) Ltd

Effective Date: 01 September 2025

Vyntara Solutions (Pty) Ltd ("Vyntara Solutions", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information collected through the iLTSM by Vyntara mobile application and related services (collectively, the "App" or "Service"). The App is distributed via the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information in compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA"), Apple App Store Review Guidelines, and Google Play Developer Program Policies.

By downloading, accessing, or using the App, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

We may collect the following personal information:

  • Name and surname
  • Email address
  • Company or organisation name
  • Account login credentials
  • Billing and subscription status (payments are processed by Apple or Google)

1.2 App Usage Information

We may collect limited technical and usage information, including:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • Log and diagnostic data (crash reports, performance metrics)

This data is used to improve app stability, performance, and user experience.

1.3 Customer Content

The App allows you to upload or manage digital asset information. You remain the owner of this content. We process this data only to provide the Service.

1.4 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not intentionally collect:

  • Sensitive personal information (e.g. race, religion, health data)
  • Precise location data
  • Contacts, photos, or files outside the App's core functionality

2. How We Use Personal Information

We use personal information to:

  • Provide and operate the App and its features
  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Communicate service-related information (e.g. updates, security notices)
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

3. App Store Payments

All payments and subscriptions are handled by:

  • Apple App Store (iOS)
  • Google Play Store (Android)

We do not collect or store payment card or banking details for in-app purchases.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share personal information only:

  • With trusted service providers (e.g. cloud hosting) under strict confidentiality obligations
  • When required by law or regulatory authorities
  • To protect our legal rights or prevent fraud or security issues

Third-party service providers may process data outside South Africa, subject to appropriate data protection safeguards.

5. Data Security and Retention

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational measures to safeguard personal information. No system is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide the Service or comply with legal requirements. Upon account termination, data may be retained for a limited period to allow data export, after which it may be deleted.

6. User Rights

In accordance with POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction or deletion of personal information
  • Object to processing in certain circumstances

Requests can be made by contacting us using the details below. Deleting certain data may limit or prevent use of the App. See our Data Deletion page for how to request deletion.

7.1 The App may be used to manage information relating to children only where such use is initiated and controlled by a parent, legal guardian, school, or authorised organisation.

7.2 We do not knowingly allow children to create accounts or use the App independently. Accounts must be created and managed by an authorised adult or organisation.

7.3 Personal information relating to children is limited to what is strictly necessary to provide the Service and may include identifiers such as name, age, or asset-related records, depending on the App's functionality.

7.4 We process children's personal information only:

  • With the consent of a parent, legal guardian, or authorised organisation; and
  • In accordance with POPIA and applicable child data protection requirements.

7.5 We do not use children's personal information for marketing or advertising purposes.

7.6 If a parent or guardian believes that a child's personal information has been collected without appropriate authorisation, they may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion.

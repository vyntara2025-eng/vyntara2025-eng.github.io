Data subject rights

Data Deletion & Removal

Vyntara Solutions (Pty) Ltd

Effective Date: 01 September 2025

This page explains how you can request deletion of your personal information and related records processed by Vyntara Solutions (Pty) Ltd ("Vyntara Solutions", "we", "us", or "our") in connection with the iLTSM by Vyntara web and mobile applications (the "Service").

We respect your rights under applicable data protection laws, including the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA"), and we provide a clear process for submitting data deletion (erasure) requests.

1. How to Request Data Deletion

To initiate a data deletion request, please contact our Data Protection team by email. This is currently the primary and official channel for requesting removal of your data from our systems.

Data Deletion / Data Subject Requests

Email: DataProtection@vyntara.co.za

Subject line: "Data Deletion Request – iLTSM"

In your email, please include the following information so that we can identify you and your data correctly:

  • Your full name and surname
  • The email address and/or mobile number associated with your account
  • The organisation or school you are linked to (if applicable)
  • A brief description of what you would like deleted (for example: user account, student records, class data)
  • Any relevant screenshots or reference numbers, if available

For security reasons, we may ask you to verify your identity or your relationship to an organisation (for example, where you are acting on behalf of a school or as an authorised administrator) before we process the request.

2. What We Can Delete

When you request deletion, we will assess your request and confirm which data can be erased. Depending on your role and how you use the Service, deletion may include:

  • Your iLTSM user account profile and login
  • Contact details stored for communication purposes
  • Student, class, or asset records that you control as a customer or school (where permitted by law and contract)
  • Associated usage data that can reasonably be linked to your account

Some information may be retained in a limited form where we have a legal or contractual obligation to do so, for example:

  • Records required for financial, tax, or audit purposes
  • Security, fraud-prevention, or abuse-prevention logs
  • Backups that are kept for disaster recovery for a limited retention period

Where full deletion is not possible, we will, where appropriate, de‑identify or anonymise data so that it can no longer be linked to a specific individual.

3. Who May Submit a Request

You may submit a data deletion request if you are:

  • An individual user of the Service; or
  • A parent or legal guardian requesting deletion of a child's information; or
  • An authorised representative of a customer organisation (for example, a school administrator).

If you are using iLTSM through a school or organisation, we may redirect your request to the relevant data controller (for example, the school) and work with them to process your request in line with our agreement and applicable law.

4. What Happens After You Submit a Request

Once we receive your email at DataProtection@vyntara.co.za:

  • We will acknowledge receipt of your request within a reasonable time.
  • We may request additional information to confirm your identity or authority.
  • We will review which data we hold and what can be deleted or de‑identified.
  • We will inform you of the outcome and the effective date of deletion or de‑identification.

In many cases, deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Complex or large‑scale requests, or requests that involve multiple systems or third‑party providers, may take longer. If we reasonably need more time, we will let you know.

5. Impact of Deleting Your Data

Deleting certain data may affect how you or your organisation can use the Service. For example:

  • Deleting your user account will remove your access to the App and related data.
  • Deleting student or class records may affect historical reports, registers, or audits.
  • Some features may no longer be available if underlying data is removed.

Before we complete a deletion request, we may ask you to confirm that you understand the impact and that you have exported or backed up any information you wish to keep.

6. Relationship with Our Privacy Policy

This Data Deletion page should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which describes in more detail what data we collect, why we collect it, and how long we keep it. Where there is any inconsistency, the Privacy Policy and applicable law will take precedence.

7. Facebook User Data Deletion Instructions

If you used Facebook Login to access the iLTSM by Vyntara application, you can request deletion of your data by following the instructions below.

Send an email to DataProtection@vyntara.co.za with the subject line "Facebook Data Deletion Request – iLTSM".

Please include your Facebook name and the email address associated with your account so we can identify your data.
